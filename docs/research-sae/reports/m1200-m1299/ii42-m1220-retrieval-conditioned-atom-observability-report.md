# M1220 Retrieval-Conditioned Atom Observability

## Question

M1219 showed that the M1217 contrastive teacher atoms are not recoverable from
baseline query-atom cooccurrence.  M1220 tests a stronger input shape:
candidate atoms generated by native proposal sources that are already
available at query time.

This is an observability audit only.  It does not replay generated postings.

## Results

Hard-row smoke: `cqadupstack`, `scidocs`, `webis-touche2020`.

| Variant | TargetRecall | Precision | HarmPrecision | AnyHit | PredCount |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `model_logistic_top8` | 1.0000 | 0.1901 | 0.3802 | 0.1606 | 1.056 |
| `model_logistic_top3` | 0.9600 | 0.1912 | 0.3705 | 0.1606 | 1.008 |

Full shared15:

| Variant | TargetRecall | Precision | HarmPrecision | AnyHit | PredCount |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `model_logistic_top8` | 1.0000 | 0.1529 | 0.1869 | 0.2548 | 3.417 |
| `model_logistic_top3` | 0.8146 | 0.1742 | 0.1752 | 0.2422 | 2.442 |

M1220 fixes the denominator bug from the initial smoke pass: queries with no
candidate atoms are now counted in the target/harm rates.

## Interpretation

M1220 is a real improvement over M1219.  Full shared15 target recall moved from
`0.0899` in baseline-query cooccurrence to `1.0000` with retrieval-conditioned
candidate atoms.

The blocker moved, but did not disappear.  Target atoms are now visible, while
harm atoms remain heavily entangled.  Top8 recovers all targets but selects more
harm than target.  Top3 improves precision but only reaches parity with harm.

## Decision

Do not replay M1220 directly.

Keep the retrieval-conditioned candidate source as a valid input shape, then
test explicit harm-aware selection before native replay.
