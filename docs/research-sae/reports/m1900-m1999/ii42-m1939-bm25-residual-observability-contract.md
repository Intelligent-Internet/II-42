# II-42 M1939 BM25-Residual Observability Contract

Date: 2026-07-13

## Objective

Determine whether a mature learned-sparse parent has a stable, query-disjoint
training signal specifically on errors that lexical retrieval does not solve.
This is the required evidence gate before any M1940 residual continuation.

M1939 does not train a model and does not use BEIR qrels. It reuses the fixed
RLHN NQ, HotpotQA, and FEVER public split and the frozen M1914, OpenSearch, and
PPLX scores generated by M1918.

## New Mechanism

The new variable is not another static sparse calibration or generic
distillation loss. It is a CLEAR-style residual margin. For each public
positive and hard-negative competition:

```text
lexical_margin = BM25(q, d+) - BM25(q, d-)
required_semantic_margin = 0.20 - lexical_margin
residual_hinge = max(
    0,
    required_semantic_margin - semantic_margin,
)
```

When BM25 already separates the positive, the semantic requirement becomes
smaller. When BM25 fails, the semantic path receives the larger requirement.
This preserves the product decomposition: lexical postings carry stable
surface evidence, while semantic postings learn residual evidence.

The principle follows CLEAR, while the product shape remains one unified
posting index rather than BM25 plus a separate ANN index:

- CLEAR: https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.13969
- BM25 query augmentation: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.14087
- DF-FLOPS: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15070
- Dual skipping guidance: https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.11154

## Surface

The fixed input is M1918 `teacher-observability-s1b-scaled`:

- 2,048 training queries;
- 512 query-disjoint heldout queries;
- NQ, HotpotQA, and FEVER only;
- public positives and RLHN hard negatives;
- frozen M1914, OpenSearch, and PPLX candidate scores.

BM25 is calculated over the union of unique documents visible on this fixed
surface. Tokenization uses the same conservative lower-cased Unicode term
extraction as the II-42 plain-query adapter. Lucene BM25 uses `k1=1.5` and
`b=0.75`.

This candidate universe is intentionally a cheap observability surface. It is
not a replacement for full-corpus native PostgreSQL evaluation.

## Measurements

Report train, heldout, and per-corpus values for:

- BM25 error rows;
- M1914, OpenSearch, and PPLX rescue conditional on a BM25 error;
- BM25 plus M1914 joint-error rows;
- PPLX rescue conditional on the joint error;
- lexical-correct rows harmed by each semantic route;
- CLEAR residual hinge and margin distributions;
- train-to-heldout stability.

## Predeclared Gate

M1940 is authorized only when all conditions pass:

1. heldout has at least 64 BM25-error rows;
2. every corpus has at least 8 heldout BM25-error rows;
3. heldout has at least 16 BM25 plus M1914 joint-error rows;
4. every corpus has at least one heldout joint-error row;
5. M1914 or PPLX rescues at least 25% of BM25 errors in every corpus;
6. PPLX rescues at least 10% of M1914 joint errors on heldout;
7. train/heldout BM25-error rates differ by at most 0.10;
8. train/heldout M1914 rescue rates differ by at most 0.15.

These floors are fixed before running M1939. Failure stops residual training
on this source; it does not justify changing thresholds or selecting a subset.

## Conditional M1940 Design

If M1939 passes, M1940 may train a small M1914 continuation with:

- frozen or minimally trainable mature Granite support;
- BM25-conditioned residual hinge;
- parent score/support protection;
- PPLX only on the residual joint-error subset;
- DF-weighted or DF-FLOPS cost control;
- checkpoint selection on the public query-disjoint heldout split.

BEIR is used only after the public gate passes. Promotion still requires the
same full native four-row gate followed by the unseen three-row transfer gate
used by M1934.

## Stop Conditions

- Stop if the predeclared observability gate fails.
- Stop if a canary improves residual heldout loss but not heldout ordering.
- Stop if native quality gain requires more than 1.125x the frozen product
  budget or increases maximum DF above the parent.
- Stop if gains are limited to one source or disappear on unseen transfer.
- Do not use dataset-specific thresholds, alpha, or checkpoint selection.
